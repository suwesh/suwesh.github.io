There are engineering problems you discover because they are difficult.

And then there are the ones you discover when you are really, really lazy.

For me, it was QA.

I was temporarily assigned GUI testing work to learn a large enterprise application.

The assignment made sense. The business application had complicated workflows, hidden dependencies, and enough business rules to punish anyone who clicked through it without understanding the process and following the correct flow, and skipping any step would result in a painful list of validations later on that would make anyone reconsider their employment.

There was only one problem.

I did not want to keep doing these testing, specially not manually.
